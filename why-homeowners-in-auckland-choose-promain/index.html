    Why Concrete Tile Roof Repairs Need Specialists

    Concrete tile roofs are popular across Auckland because they’re durable, stylish, and can last for decades. But like any part of your home, they need proper care.

    Over time, the harsh New Zealand climate can take a toll:

    • Tiles can crack or break during storms.
    • Lichen and moss can grow, lifting tiles and allowing water to seep in.
    • Mortar in the ridge caps can deteriorate, causing loose tiles and gaps.

    A common mistake homeowners make is hiring someone who doesn’t specialise in concrete tile roof repairs. Without the right skills, tools, and understanding of how these roofs work, repairs can actually make the problem worse – just like in Helen’s case.

    Promain’s team knows concrete tile roofs inside out. They can spot problems others might miss and provide solutions that keep your roof strong for years to come.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I know if my concrete tile roof needs repairs?

    Look for cracked or missing tiles, moss or lichen growth, leaks inside your home, or loose ridge caps. If you’re unsure, Promain can carry out a professional inspection.

    Can you repair just a few broken tiles, or do I need a full roof replacement?

    In most cases, individual tiles can be replaced without needing to redo the entire roof. Promain will assess the damage and recommend the most cost-effective solution.

    How often should I get my roof cleaned?

    In Auckland’s climate, a professional roof clean every 2–3 years can help prevent moss and lichen build-up, which can damage tiles over time.

    Will repairing my roof add value to my home?

    Absolutely. A well-maintained roof improves your home’s street appeal, prevents water damage, and reassures potential buyers that the property has been cared for.
