    • Boat Detailing in Auckland & the Coromandel – My Experience with Quay Pro

    When you’ve owned a boat for a few years, you realise something very quickly: boating is pure joy… but boat cleaning? Not so much. That’s where I discovered one of the best investments I’ve made in looking after my pride and joy – Quay Pro Boat Detailing.

    I’ve been using Matthew and his team for a couple of years now. I’m based between Auckland and Whangamata, with my boat spending time both in the marina and on a trailer. Over time, I’ve come to see Quay Pro not just as a service provider, but as the people who make sure I can enjoy my boat without the endless scrubbing, polishing, and maintenance stress that comes with boat ownership.

    Why I Chose Quay Pro

    I first found Quay Pro when I’d been out on the water for an epic day of fishing and family time. The boat was covered in salt, the deck was sticky from bait, and honestly, the thought of spending the next few hours cleaning it felt like the quickest way to ruin the day. A fellow boatie at Whangamata Marina suggested I give Matthew a call, mentioning “once you start with him, you’ll never go back to doing it yourself.”

    He wasn’t wrong. From that first visit, I could tell Matthew’s approach was different. It wasn’t just about making the boat look good for now; it was about protecting my marine asset for the long term. As someone who’s seen the effects of the harsh Kiwi climate – the sun bleaching gelcoat, the salt corroding stainless fittings, the mildew creeping into upholstery – that kind of thinking made perfect sense.

    A Service That Travels With You

    One of the biggest advantages for me is that Quay Pro is mobile. Whether my boat’s in Auckland, Pauanui, Tairua, or Whangamata, Matthew comes to me. That’s not just convenient – it’s game-changing. I no longer have to plan long cleaning sessions at home or try to fit in time at a yard. Wherever the boat is, Quay Pro makes it look as though it’s just rolled out of a showroom.

    What They Actually Do For My Boat

    Over the years, I’ve used most of their services, and I can honestly say each one makes a difference.

    1. Full Exterior Detailing and Polishing

    This is the bread and butter of Quay Pro’s work – the deep cleans that strip away grime, salt, and stubborn stains, followed by polishing and waxing that makes the hull shine and keeps it protected. Matthew’s eye for detail is sharp. He doesn’t miss the small spots around cleats or in hard-to-reach corners.

    2. Stainless Polishing

    If you’ve ever owned a boat, you know that stainless fittings don’t stay shiny forever. In our salty environment, they can dull and start to oxidise quickly. Quay Pro’s stainless polishing has brought mine back to mirror finish time and time again.

    3. Teak Cleaning and Oiling

    I’ve got teak decking, and before using Quay Pro, it was starting to look grey and tired. Their specialised teak cleaning products strip away the grime, and the oiling restores that rich, warm colour I fell in love with when I bought the boat.

    4. Interior Cleaning

    It’s not just the outside that gets attention. After long periods of storage – especially over winter – Matthew has given the interior a complete freshen up. Carpets cleaned, upholstery spotless, and any musty smells gone.

    5. Engine Room Cleans

    I never thought I’d get excited about my engine room, but here we are. Oil, grease, and grime have no place there, and keeping it clean actually makes it easier to spot potential issues early.

    Why I Keep Coming Back

    There are a few reasons why I’ve become a repeat customer – and why I’ve referred half a dozen mates to Matthew.

    1. Consistency – Every job is done to the same high standard.
    2. Trust – I’m happy to hand over the keys knowing my boat’s in good hands.
    3. Custom Packages – My needs change depending on the season, and Matthew tailors the service to match.
    4. Local Knowledge – He knows the specific conditions in Auckland and the Coromandel, so he knows what to look for and how to prevent damage.

    The Difference a Professional Makes

    Sure, anyone can grab a bucket and sponge, but professional detailing is another level. Matthew uses superior products, techniques, and tools that protect the finish, extend the lifespan of materials, and make the boat easier to maintain between services. Plus, the value of my boat has been preserved – something I’ll thank myself for if I ever decide to sell.

    A Typical Visit

    Here’s how it usually goes:

    • I call or message Matthew to book in.
    • He confirms a time and location (trailer, berth, or yard).
    • On the day, he arrives with everything he needs.
    • He works methodically, often spending hours ensuring every surface is cleaned, polished, or treated.
    • I turn up at the end, step aboard, and it’s like having a new boat all over again.

    It’s the easiest part of boat ownership.

    FAQs – From One Boat Owner to Another

    Do I really need professional boat detailing if I clean it myself?

    Yes. I still give my boat a quick rinse after a trip, but professional detailing goes deeper, removing oxidation, salt deposits, and stains you can’t get rid of with a simple wash. It also adds protective coatings that make the next clean easier.

    How often should I get my boat detailed?

    It depends on how often you use it and where it’s stored. I personally book Matthew every few months in summer and once before and after winter storage.

    Will they come to my marina or home?

    Absolutely. Whether my boat’s at a marina in Auckland, on a trailer at home, or in Whangamata, Matthew comes to me. That’s part of what makes the service so easy.

    Can they restore older boats?

    Yes. I’ve seen Matthew take a dull, faded hull and make it shine again. Their enhancement and restoration work is ideal if your boat’s looking tired.

    What makes Quay Pro different from other cleaners?

    It’s the experience, the mobile service, the quality of products, and the fact that Matthew genuinely cares about protecting your asset – not just making it look good for a day.

    If you’re based in Auckland or anywhere across the Coromandel – Pauanui, Tairua, Whangamata – and you want to spend more time enjoying your boat and less time scrubbing it, I can’t recommend Quay Pro Boat Detailing enough.
